📱 Mobile Touch & Swipe Gestures

👆👆

Double Touch / Double Tap

Television / IPTV / Video: Toggles Full Screen mode on mobile device.
Mobile Reels: Likes the video with floating heart animation.

👈👉

Swipe Left / Swipe Right

Television & IPTV: Swiping left/right switches channels (Next/Previous).
Gallery: Swiping switches tabs and views.

👆👇

Swipe Up / Swipe Down

Mobile Reels Feed: Swiping up/down moves to the next or previous video loop.
Lock Screen: Swipes up to unlock device.

📜

Mobile Portrait Scrolling

In portrait view on mobile, scroll down smoothly to view generated cards and preview panels stacked below the input forms.

⌨️ PC Keyboard Controls

F

Toggle Fullscreen

Press F key on any video player or simulator app to enter/exit native browser full screen.

M

Mute / Unmute Audio

Press M to toggle global audio mute state instantly across stream players.

← →

Left / Right Arrows

Switch channels on Television/IPTV or navigate items horizontally.

↑ ↓

Up / Down Arrows

Scroll vertical reels video loops or change TV channels.

Esc

Escape / Close

Exit focused player view, close configuration dialogs, or return to previous screen.
